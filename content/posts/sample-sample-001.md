Agentic AI systems are forcing us to rethink software architecture from the ground up. Instead of a single request/response cycle, we now design for agents that plan, call tools, and recover from failure autonomously. The teams that win here treat the agent loop itself as a first-class architectural component, not an afterthought bolted onto an existing service. #AgenticAI #SoftwareArchitecture #AI
