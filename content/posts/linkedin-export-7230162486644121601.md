It is super important to recognise the value of this mindset in making progress without getting stuck. However, it is equally important to know when to dive deeper and ensure that your thoughts resurface in different contexts. This conscious effort can amplify positive outcomes and make sure your insights are heard and embraced by others. Otherwise, you risk working in isolation, which may hinder your ability to play a collaborative role—an essential factor in achieving anything truly valuable.
