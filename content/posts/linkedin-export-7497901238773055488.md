Really enjoyed this six-session Wise Wednesday journey with Agent Boutique AI"
""
"What started as a small idea at home turned into an opportunity to go much deeper into Speech AI, learn along the way, and share that learning with the team."
""
"The idea came from my son wanting to write “I went to school” in his diary. In his head, "went" was spelled "vnt", and he kept asking his mother for help."
""
"I told him, "It’s your personal diary. Just write whatever you want. Spelling mistakes are okay.""
""
"But I also wanted to solve the underlying problem without anyone else having to enter his little diary world."
""
"That became Little Voices, made with love for kids, built with Claude, but guided by completely human taste. In this case, mine."
""
"From there, the Little Voices demo became the starting point for six Wednesdays of exploring Speech AI from the ground up: audio processing, Whisper, live transcription, streaming architectures, and eventually Moonshine v2."
""
"One question that kept us particularly curious was:"
""
"Why does a live transcript feel slower the longer someone talks?"
""
"Going deep enough to find the answer was one of my favourite parts of the journey."
""
"I am grateful to Agent Boutique AI for creating the space and giving me the opportunity to explore, experiment, and share this with the team. That is what I really like about the Wise Wednesday idea: it gives us permission to go beyond “knowing about” something and actually build a deeper understanding together."
""
"Thank you to everyone #ABAI-Team who participated, asked questions, challenged ideas, and made these sessions so much fun."
""
"And if you are working with real-time transcription, voice assistants, or streaming audio, the Whisper vs. Moonshine v2 comparison in this series is worth a look."
""
"Always grateful for opportunities where learning and sharing become the same thing."
""
