Whenever I'm unsure how to split a service boundary, I ask: which side of this line changes more often? Put the volatile logic behind the interface that changes least. It's not a universal law, but it has resolved more architecture debates on my teams than any diagram. #SoftwareEngineering #SystemDesign #Architecture
