While cleaning up old photos to free up some storage, I stumbled upon a few memories that took me back to one of the defining moments of my early career."
""
"A welder's apron."
"A real welding torch."
"And a virtual welding simulator."
"And an AR-assisted welding shield that we built as the next evolution of the product."
""
"Back in 2013, I had the opportunity to demonstrate a welding training simulator to ministers at ITI, Tinsukia, Assam."
""
"But what stayed with me was not the demo."
""
"It was everything that happened before it."
""
"Our team knew that building a good simulator meant understanding welding first."
""
"We spent time with welders on the shop floor, learning how they worked and the challenges they faced."
""
"We also learned from researchers, trainers, and industry practitioners and other institutions that had deep expertise in welding."
""
"Only after immersing ourselves in the domain did we begin building the technology."
""
"The result was a safe and cost-effective VR/AR welding simulator that allowed trainees to practice without consuming materials or exposing themselves to unnecessary risk."
""
"Looking back, one of the things I cherish most is the team behind it."
""
"We challenged each other's ideas, learned from experts, and built something that none of us could have created alone."
""
"I am grateful to everyone who was part of that journey."
""
"That project quietly changed something fundamental in me."
""
"It taught me that technology itself is rarely the innovation."
""
""The real innovation is developing a deep understanding of a problem and then crafting technology that fits naturally into its solution.""
""
"More than a decade later, that lesson still shapes how I approach every project."
"Whether it is AI, agentic systems, or software engineering, I still start the same way."
""
"Learn the domain."
"Listen to the people who live the problem every day."
"Then build the technology."
""
"Looking back, I realize this project was not just one of my first innovations."
""
"It was where I developed an engineering mindset that has stayed with me ever since."
""
""Sometimes the old photos we almost delete remind us why we do what we do.""
""
