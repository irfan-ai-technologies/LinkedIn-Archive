One question won't stop finding me."
""
"In my AI cohort. At NGI Academy sessions. From students, friends, and family."
""
""Would you still tell someone to enter software with AI moving this fast? Claude, Codex, Devin...""
""
"My answer has always been the same."
"Yes."
""
"Not because software engineering is safe from AI. Because it was never just about writing code. AI coding assistants are just making that more obvious than ever."
""
"Coding is simply one way we solve problems. The real work is:"
"• Understanding people and the environment they live or work in "
"• Spotting opportunities where others see friction "
"• Making decisions under constraints "
"• Weighing trade-offs with no perfect answer"
""
"Every meaningful solution is a series of trade-offs."
"> Speed or quality? "
"> Scale or simplicity? "
"> Cost or customer experience? "
"> Privacy or convenience?"
""
"There is rarely a perfect answer. Only better judgment."
""
"And here is what I think matters most in the age of AI: "judgment can't be downloaded.""
""
"Take a production outage."
"You can read postmortems. Learn from experienced engineers. Ask AI to simulate the entire incident. All of that is valuable."
"But none of it compares to being the person responsible when production is actually down."
"• When customers are impacted "
"• When your teammates are looking to you "
"• When every minute matters "
"• When there isn't a perfect answer, only trade-offs"
"That is where judgment is built."
""
"The same is true beyond engineering."
"--> You don't build product instinct from product books. "
"--> You don't build leadership by reading about leadership. "
"--> You don't build engineering judgment by writing better prompts."
""
""Knowledge expands your options. Ownership sharpens your judgment. ""
""
"Judgment is earned only by carrying real responsibility. Ironically, AI makes this more valuable, not less. As intelligence becomes abundant, judgment becomes scarce."
""
"So here is my advice:"
"• If you are a student: Don't chase tools. Chase real problems. "
"• If you are already building: Don't measure your growth by the AI tools you have mastered. Measure it by the quality of decisions people trust you to make. "
"• And even if you never write a line of code: Build the habit of noticing problems, thinking clearly, and taking ownership. "
""
"Those skills compound for a lifetime."
"The future won't belong to the person who generates the most code."
"It will belong to the person who consistently makes the better call."
""
""Judgment may become the rarest skill in the age of intelligence.""
""
